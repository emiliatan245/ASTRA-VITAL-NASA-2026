This statement is intentionally comprehensive. Do not shorten it in a way that hides AI involvement.

The participant must review and approve the final work and must not claim that unperformed tests or analyses were completed.

## Machine-learning model used by the application

The application includes a scikit-learn RandomForestClassifier trained offline on 18,000 synthetic sensor-scenario rows generated by `generate_model.py`. The input features and target labels are artificial. The model is serialised to `model.js` and runs locally in the browser. It does not call a hosted AI service at runtime.

The classifier predicts only three synthetic scenario labels (`routine_pattern`, `review_pattern`, `priority_review_pattern`). Its reported holdout accuracy and macro-F1 are measures of agreement with artificial labels from the synthetic scenario generator. They are not medical accuracy or evidence of clinical validity. The model is not trained on NASA astronaut medical records, does not diagnose, prescribe, assess medical fitness, or determine emergency urgency.

## Critical NASA policy clarification

The 2026 NASA Space Apps Project Submission Guide permits SI tools as a means to accelerate innovation and creativity but says SI tools should not be used to come up with the idea of how to solve the challenge. ChatGPT did assist with initial solution framing and feature ideas in this conversation. The participant has been advised to disclose this accurately to the Kharagpur Local Lead and obtain clarification before submitting. This document does not assert that the project is automatically eligible; the Local Lead / organisers must advise.

## Synthetic data disclosure

All 18,000 model training records were generated programmatically by `generate_model.py` with a fixed random seed. The scenario labels were generated by the same script. The small built-in dashboard time series is also synthetic. No private astronaut medical records or real sensor feed was used to train the model. Synthetic data is clearly marked in the application and CSV.

## At runtime

The serialized Random Forest classifier executes in the browser. Check-in and routine data remain in browser local storage. User-selected CSV/TSV research files are read and parsed locally by the browser; ASTRA-VITAL does not upload them to a server. There is no live sensor connection, NASA API feed, remote clinical model, or automated treatment system.
