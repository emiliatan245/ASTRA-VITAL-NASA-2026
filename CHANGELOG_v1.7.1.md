# ASTRA-VITAL v1.7.1 — Named anatomical pointers

- Removed numbered circular badges from the anatomy overlay.
- Replaced badges with small structure-anchored pointer pins, leader lines, and visible anatomical labels.
- Labels now name structures (Brain, Lungs, Heart, Liver & kidneys, Pelvis & legs, Spinal cord) and adapt to the selected anatomy layer.
- Alternating left/right callouts reduce label collisions; labels adapt near viewer edges.
- Keyboard-focus, hover, and selected states are retained.
- Updated helper text, canvas accessibility label, and the body-system information panel to refer to named anatomical pointers rather than numbered markers.
- No model data, synthetic training data, user records, or measurement logic was changed.
