## Important limitation

All examples and labels were generated by this script. The model learns to reproduce synthetic scenario labels. Holdout metrics measure synthetic-label agreement only; they do not establish clinical validity, real astronaut performance, safety, or readiness for medical decision-making. Do not use the model for real health decisions.
