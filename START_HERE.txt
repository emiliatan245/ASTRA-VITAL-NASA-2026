ASTRA-VITAL v1.7.1

RECOMMENDED (Windows):
1. Keep this whole folder together.
2. Make sure you have an internet connection for the detailed anatomy model.
3. Double-click START_ASTRA_VITAL.bat.
4. The app opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:8765/.
5. Open Anatomy Explorer, select Male or Female, and choose a body layer. Named pointers identify the visible structures; click a label to open its information panel.

The actual Open Anatomy 3D male/female model downloads from jsDelivr on first use.
If the connection is unavailable or the browser blocks the model, ASTRA-VITAL displays its simplified procedural 3D fallback.
