# ASTRA-VITAL v1.3.0 release notes

### Visual refresh
- Deep black / navy / electric-blue palette.
- Frosted, translucent panels and soft blue focus rings.
- Scrollable welcome / mission briefing with challenge, solution and scientific boundaries.
- Subtle CSS rocket and astronaut animation; automatically disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Anatomy explorer
- Step 1: choose Male reference, Female reference, or Neutral reference.
- Step 2: choose Normal body, Nervous system, Organs & circulation, Skeleton, or Muscle system.
- Six clickable body pointers continue to open the Overview / Spaceflight / My concern information panel.
- The visual-reference choice does not change medical thresholds, model outputs, or data.

### Preserved functionality
- Mission console, crew assessment, synthetic Random Forest model, mission routine, daily log, health trends, research library, NASA data lab, and model card.
- Existing full-card external research links.
- Existing local observation records remain in the browser's local storage when updating in place.

### Important limits
- This is an educational prototype, not a clinical device or approved flight system.
- Demonstration telemetry and model labels are synthetic.
- Anatomy graphics are illustrative and not medical scans.
